Executive Summary</h2><p>This project delivers an end-to-end analytical framework for insurance claims data, focusing on two critical business dimensions: <strong>time to resolution</strong> and <strong>anomaly/fraud detection</strong>. Using synthetic data generated with Python and analyzed in R Studio, the project provides actionable insights for claims operations optimization and risk management.</p><h3>1.1 Key Business Impact</h3><ul> <li><strong>Operational Efficiency</strong>: Identified segments with resolution times up to 60 days (Health-Medium claims), revealing clear process improvement opportunities.</li> <li><strong>Risk Management</strong>: Detected concentrated fraud indicators in specific client segments, enabling targeted investigation.</li> <li><strong>Data-Driven Strategy</strong>: Established severity as the primary driver of resolution time, supporting segmented claims management approaches.</li> </ul><hr><h2>2. Project Objectives</h2><ol> <li>Measure and analyze the average time to resolution of insurance claims.</li> <li>Detect anomalies and potential fraud indicators using statistical and behavioral patterns.</li> <li>Provide visual insights for operational decision-making and risk prioritization.</li> </ol><hr><h2>3. Analytical Approaches</h2><h3>9.1 Statistical Methods</h3> <ul> <li><strong>Descriptive Statistics</strong>: Mean, median, standard deviation for resolution times</li> <li><strong>Distribution Analysis</strong>: Density plots, boxplots by category</li> <li><strong>Time Series</strong>: Monthly trend analysis with smoothing</li> <li><strong>Outlier Detection</strong>: Statistical thresholds (IQR, percentiles)</li> </ul><h3>9.2 Fraud Detection Framework</h3> <ol> <li><strong>Rapid Resolution</strong>: Claims closed &lt;5 days (potential collusion)</li> <li><strong>High Frequency</strong>: &gt;5 claims per client in 30 days</li> <li><strong>Extreme Amounts</strong>: &gt;95th percentile claimed amount</li> <li><strong>Amount Disparity</strong>: Claimed &gt;80% of insured value</li> <li><strong>Severity Flags</strong>: Critical severity + high amount combinations</li> </ol><h3>9.3 Scoring System</h3> <ul> <li>Each indicator: 5-25 points</li> <li>Total risk score: 0-100</li> <li>Priority levels: Critical (&gt;45), High (35-44), Medium (25-34), Low (&lt;25)</li> </ul><hr><h2>10.
