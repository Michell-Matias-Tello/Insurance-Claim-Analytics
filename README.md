Business Overview</h2><p>This intelligence platform delivers comprehensive claims analytics with dual focus on <strong>operational efficiency</strong> and <strong>risk anomaly detection</strong>. Built on synthetic data generated through Python and analyzed using R, the solution transforms raw claims data into actionable business insights.</p><h3 id="ch1-1">1.1 Value Proposition</h3><ul> <li><strong>Efficiency Optimization</strong>: Uncovered 60-day resolution bottlenecks in Health-Medium claims, enabling targeted process improvements</li> <li><strong>Risk Mitigation</strong>: Identified high-concentration fraud patterns (Client #12 with 6 claims), enabling focused investigative resources</li> <li><strong>Data Intelligence</strong>: Validated severity as primary resolution predictor, supporting tiered operational strategies</li> <li><strong>Performance Standards</strong>: Established cross-insurance benchmarks for claims processing excellence</li> <li><strong>Preventive Controls</strong>: Deployed scoring system for early risk identification</li> </ul><h3 id="ch1-2">1.2 Solution Components</h3><ul> <li>End-to-end R analysis pipeline</li> <li>Python synthetic data engine</li> <li>Interactive visualization layer</li> <li>Executive decision support dashboard</li> <li>Comprehensive data documentation</li> <li>Fraud risk assessment toolkit</li> </ul><hr><h2 id="ch2">2. Analytical Framework</h2><h3 id="ch9-1">9.1 Quantitative Techniques</h3> <ul> <li><strong>Descriptive Statistics</strong>: Mean, median, standard deviation for resolution times</li> <li><strong>Distribution Analysis</strong>: Density plots, boxplots by category</li> <li><strong>Time Series</strong>: Monthly trend analysis with smoothing</li> <li><strong>Outlier Detection</strong>: Statistical thresholds (IQR, percentiles)</li> <li><strong>Heteroscedasticity Analysis</strong>: Variance assessment across segments</li> </ul><h3 id="ch9-2">9.2 Fraud Pattern Recognition</h3> <ol> <li><strong>Rapid Resolution</strong>: Claims closed less than 5 days (potential collusion)</li> <li><strong>High Frequency</strong>: More than 5 claims per client in 30 days</li> <li><strong>Extreme Amounts</strong>: Greater than 95th percentile claimed amount</li> <li><strong>Amount Disparity</strong>: Claimed more than 80% of insured value</li> <li><strong>Severity Flags</strong>: Critical severity + high amount combinations</li> <li><strong>Network Analysis</strong>: Connected components and centrality metrics</li> </ol><h3 id="ch9-3">9.3 Risk Quantification</h3> <ul> <li><strong>Each indicator</strong>: 5-25 points</li> <li><strong>Total risk score</strong>: 0-100</li> <li><strong>Priority levels</strong>: <ul> <li>Critical: Greater than 45</li> <li>High: 35-44</li> <li>Medium: 25-34</li> <li>Low: Less than 25</li> </ul> </li> </ul><hr><h2 id="ch10">10.
